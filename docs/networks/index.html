Networks

Dash Forge runs on Dash Platform. Each network has its own identities, repositories and DASH, and nothing moves between them. The web app shows the same table at forge.dashhq.org/networks, read from the deployment files in forge-contracts/deployments/.

Where Forge runs today

NetworkStatusNotes
MainnetLive since 2026-11-24forge.dashhq.org. Dash Platform v5 (protocol 14), chain id evo1. Forge's four contracts (forge-core, forge-collab, forge-community, forge-meta) are registered in one contract group, H5gXhGVabbSdDdZMesVXevfbvNU8CgSCcHdhx6ekL2eJ. Real DASH.
TestnetLive since 2026-10-26testnet-forge.dashhq.org. Dash Platform v5 (protocol 14), chain id dash-testnet-51, contract group EXgzBMpwC7RMjSU4WQghLAnfboswsssMGiXRtiV1hmBC. DASH is free test money from the Dash testnet faucet or faucet.thepasta.org. Testnet can be reset, and everything on it would go with it except your git clones and your own storage.

dg and git-remote-dash default to testnet until you pick a network: dg auth login --network mainnet (or dg auth new --network mainnet) records mainnet as your default. The v0.2.0 binaries work on testnet only and have no --wallet; for mainnet or wallet sign-in, build from source. On a network with no Forge deployment, dg, git-remote-dash and the web app stop with a "not deployed" error (E702). Used Forge on a devnet before testnet? Dash Forge moved to testnet says what was kept and how to push again.

History

NetworkDatesWhat happened
Mainnet2026-11-24 to nowForge's four contracts were registered on mainnet from the same JSON as testnet, and forge.dashhq.org moved to mainnet. The testnet site moved to testnet-forge.dashhq.org. The registration evidence is in forge-contracts/evidence/mainnet/.
Testnet2026-10-26 to nowThe production four-contract set (forge-core, forge-collab, forge-community, forge-meta) was registered on testnet (Platform v5.0.0-rc.8, protocol 14) by a dedicated deployer, from the same JSON later registered on mainnet. forge.dashhq.org, dg and the guides moved to testnet. Nothing was carried over from sakura: create your identity again and re-push from your clone (how). The registration evidence is in forge-contracts/evidence/testnet/.
Devnet sakura (down)2026-10-24Sakura's nodes stopped answering around 2026-10-24 and its DNS names were removed. It is not coming back, and everything Forge kept there is gone; your git clones and your own storage were not affected.
Devnet sakura (four-contract set)2026-10-17 to 2026-10-24Forge moved sakura to the release's four-contract set (forge-core, forge-collab, forge-community, forge-meta), the set later registered on testnet, and re-seeded the demo fixtures on it.
Devnet sakura (after its third reset)2026-10-13 to 2026-10-17On 2026-10-13 sakura's Platform chain was reset a third time, onto Platform v5.0.0-beta.6, and everything Forge kept there went with it again. Forge's contracts were registered again the same day with the same ids as before, from the same schema. Repositories, issues, pull requests and your own identities and their balances were not carried over, so create your identity again; the demo fixtures were seeded again. Your git clones and your own storage were not affected.
Devnet sakura (after its second reset)2026-10-08 to 2026-10-13On 2026-10-08 sakura's Platform chain was reset again, onto Platform v5.0.0-beta.3, and everything Forge kept there went with it again. Forge's contracts were registered again the same day with the same ids as before (the deployer was re-created from its original funding), from the same schema. Repositories, issues, pull requests and your own identities and their balances were not carried over, so create your identity again; the demo fixtures were seeded again. Your git clones and your own storage were not affected.
Devnet sakura (after its first reset)2026-10-07 to 2026-10-08On 2026-10-06 sakura's Platform chain was reset onto Platform v5.0.0-beta.2, and everything Forge kept there went with it: repositories, issues, pull requests, identities and their balances. Your git clones and your own storage were not affected. Forge's contracts were registered again on 2026-10-07 with new ids, from the same schema.
Devnet sakura2026-10-01 to 2026-10-06The second release candidate of the contracts (RC2) was registered on Platform v5.0.0-beta.1. Sakura replaced bonsia. Forge's three contracts (forge-core, forge-collab, forge-community) are registered in one contract group.
Devnet bonsia2026-09-29 to 2026-10-01The first release candidate (RC1, Platform v4.2.0-beta.7, tag contracts-rc1-frozen) was registered here. The web app ran on bonsia from 2026-09-30. The devnet was retired and everything on it is gone: Dash Forge moved to devnet sakura says what was lost, what was kept and how to push again.
Devnet moutaito 2026-09-29Moutai was upgraded in place to Platform v4.2.0-beta.7, which made the contracts registered there unreadable. Forge left it.

The first version of Forge (forge-v1) was removed on 2026-09-26 with no backwards compatibility.
